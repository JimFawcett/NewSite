1. Where the Damage Lands

This page draws on Global_Liabilities_of_LLM_AIs.docx, which sorts the liabilities into eight areas and matches them against the benefit list on the previous page. One pattern runs through all eight. The same low cost that puts a tutor or a translator in everyone’s hands also puts a forger, a scammer, and a confident wrong answer there. Scale cuts both ways, and the people who absorb the damage are rarely the people who chose to deploy the model.

2. Misinformation and Trust

The model writes fluent text whether or not the content is true, and it writes as much of it as anyone asks for.

5. Bias and Fairness

6. Security and Misuse

7. Privacy and Data Rights

9. Human and Social Effects

11. What Reduces the Risk

The source document ends on a qualified note: safeguards, regulation, transparency, and human oversight each cut these risks. How much they cut depends on how the technology is deployed, which returns the question to the people doing the deploying.

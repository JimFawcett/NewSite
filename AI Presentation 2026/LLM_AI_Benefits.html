1. Where the Gains Show Up

This page draws on Benefits of LLM AIs at the global and societal level.docx, which sorts the gains into seven areas. Each area below names who gains and what they get. One pattern runs through all seven. The model supplies, at low cost and on demand, a kind of help that used to require a person’s time: a tutor, a translator, a clerk, a first-draft writer. Where that help was already scarce, the gain is largest.

9. What the Gains Depend On

Most of these benefits are potential rather than guaranteed. Three conditions decide whether they arrive.
  1. Access. The gain requires an internet connection, a device, and support for the reader’s language. People without all three get none of it.
  2. Accuracy safeguards. The model states a wrong answer in the same confident wording it uses for a right one, so someone has to check the output before it reaches a patient, a student, or a filing.
  3. Deployment choices. Who gets the tool, at what price, and under what rules decides whether the gain reaches the people in each list above or stops short of them.

Human vs LLM Analogy

Analogy Take Aways

Training human brains lasts over a life-time. Training LLM models is an enormously expensive one time process. The models cannot be used until that is complete: What Are Data Centers Doing? A Mind-Boggling Amount of Math. - The New York Times Note: in an LLM conversation all of the context is sent along with the current prompt with every request. The remote models do not store any information between requests. There is one very significant processing difference. The human brain modifies long term memory with parts of its short term memory flow.
The LLM models do not do that. Allowing query context to modify trained data opens up the possibility of unintended or intended corruption of the training data with bad information. That, of course, happens with humans too. That may be why we have cults and misinformation.

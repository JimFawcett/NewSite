1. What the Presentation Covers

Artifical Inteligence models have be developed to play grand master level chess and go. Others have learned to create protein folding and weather prediction models that are as good as, or better, than existing methods. In this presentation we will be looking at a subset of AI models called Large Language Models (LLMs). they:
  • answer questions about a large corpus of knowledge that may include much of the web, portals holding scientific papers and code, forum sites and Wikipedia
  • help building slide decks, pdf documents, and web pages
  • Carry out tasks like testing code and sorting email
Specifically we cover:
  • how people like you and businesses like ClarkLinsey use LLM AIs
  • What models cost and benefits and deficits that are inherent to LLM AIs
The presentation will survey the surface of these but cannot burrow too deeply into details.

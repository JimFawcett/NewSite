1. Why should you care about AI?

You've heard a lot about Artificial Intelligence (AI) in the news and your email news letters. We are told that it will change how we live and work. AI is powerful and scary. It can be the agent for much good and much bad. Its path is uncertain but it is coming. Pandora's box has opened. AI can take a lot of the friction out of maintenance tasks for you and your surroundings. It can explain technology, help you with settings, review your medications ... It can also be fun to use AI to dig up facts about the movies over dinner. You want to know enough to make good voting choices, and good conversation.

2. What the Presentation Covers

Artificial Intelligence models have been developed to play grand master level chess and go. Others have learned to create protein folding and weather prediction models that are as good as, or better, than existing methods. In this presentation we will be looking at a subset of AI models called Large Language Models (LLMs). they:
  • answer questions about a large corpus of knowledge that may include much of the web, portals holding scientific papers and code, forum sites and Wikipedia
  • help building slide decks, pdf documents, and web pages
  • Carry out tasks like testing code and sorting email
Specifically we cover:
  • how people like you and businesses like ClarkLinsey use LLM AIs
  • What models cost, and the benefits and liabilities that are inherent to LLM AIs
This presentation will provide as much content as time permits.
